# Wall boundaries — implemented in dev

## October 2: Position-aware painted wall connections

The eight new user snips exposed that calibrated whole-image offsets and small sleeves did not reliably join unequal generated corner arms. Active rendering now assembles intact corners, perimeter Ts, centered Ts and crosses from clipped sections of the material's existing straight-wall texture. Length is clipped; thickness and image aspect ratio remain unchanged. Original join PNGs remain preserved but their main artwork is hidden for these assembled parts. The underlying terrain object, targeting, health and boundaries are unchanged.

Corners follow both rotated edge centerlines. Perimeter Ts follow the outside edge and meet a centered divider; centered Ts/crosses use the tile center. Short ends attach toward the neighboring segment (right before rotation), using a measured end offset. Damaged corners keep their original broken center, receive independent alignment and extend only the surviving outer arms. Their debris is not painted over. Existing inward-corner translation, destruction cleanup and breach calibration remain intact. This fixes presentation without regenerating maps or saves.

Installer metadata now includes wall thickness, terminal-end alignment and damaged-corner alignment. The enlarged comparison tool covers six connection types and all four rotations for each material; Battle Lab still exposes all 16 full layouts. Validation: 109 frontend tests, frontend build, seven targeted backend boundary/showcase tests, enlarged four-material browser checks and the 16-layout Battle Lab browser checks pass. Full backend baseline remains the earlier 325 tests; this pass changes no combat rules. Production untouched.

## October 2: Corner alignment and short-arm joins

The material packs have unequal generated arm lengths. The renderer now aligns a corner's horizontal and vertical arms independently using calibrated `corner_offset`, and places short, clipped sections of the same straight-wall image beneath its two connection ends. T stems get a matching sleeve at the divider seam. Images preserve their proportions and beam thickness; no stretched corner or replacement art is generated. Destroying the parent removes its sleeves. These are presentation-only pieces, not additional walls, health pools or collision barriers. Rotation and inward-corner placement follow the parent. Existing saved battles receive this rendering fix after refreshing the client.

The installer writes identical backend/frontend geometry manifests. An enlarged comparison page is generated by `tools/build_wall_join_preview.py` in staging-terrain/building-toolset-v2/join-preview.html; choose any of four materials and toggle Show repaired joins. `tools/wall_join_browser_qa.mjs` verifies actual CSS clipping and saves enlarged screenshots. Validation: 106 frontend tests, frontend build, enlarged previews in all four materials and all authored-map browser checks pass. Gameplay was unchanged; previous 323 backend tests remain the baseline.

An edge wall leaves its tile's interior floor usable, but blocks crossing the named edge in either direction. A corner closes both outside edges. A centered wall or room divider still occupies its complete tile. A divider meeting an outside wall uses a T-junction; its centered stem occupies that tile. These rules apply to new reusable building plans and rectangular enclosures. Legacy saved structures without boundary metadata retain their existing full-tile collision.

Movement, reachability, path previews, melee attacks, ranged attacks and spell line-of-effect checks share the boundary rule. Diagonal sight lines cannot slip through a closed corner. Existing magic elevation/accuracy behavior is unchanged. Walls remain attackable from either side, including from their interior floor tile; targeting a wall does not treat that wall as a barrier to striking itself.

An edge door/gate can be operated from its interior tile or the tile immediately across its boundary. Closing it does not reject a unit standing on the interior floor. Centered doors still require adjacent operation and refuse closing over an occupant. Open and destroyed gates stop blocking crossing and sight. Enemy pursuit and panic escape routes compare detours with spending an action opening a gate, and stop before crossing a closed gate. No instant opening or crossing occurs.

Data: `wall_edges` lists north/east/south/west boundaries. `edge_wall: true` exempts a structure from full-tile occupancy and sight blocking; its active edges still block. A T-junction uses edges plus full-tile occupancy. Inward decorative joins may have no cardinal edges: the adjoining perimeter segments close the exposed outside cells. Placement rotates edge directions together with the building and its artwork.

Art: four complete material-specific 4×4 atlases in `staging-terrain/building-toolset-v2`, generated using the built-in image tool. Each contains straight wall, corner, T, cross, end, breach, closed/open small door, closed/open gate, slit-window wall, pillar, stairs, damaged corner, perimeter T and brace. Runtime pieces are in `frontend/public/assets/combat-terrain/structures/building-v2`. Pillars, windows, crosses, steps and braces are available building tools; this pass adds no new interactions for them. Source art, extraction report and exact prompts remain alongside the atlases. Terrain and furniture are separate packs.

Install: `.venv\Scripts\python.exe tools\install_material_building_toolsets.py`. Extraction recovers complete alpha silhouettes, preserves their proportions, shares fixed-post anchors between door states, and calibrates corner/T positions against each family. Old libraries are preserved and stable sprite IDs select the new files. The old building installer delegates to the current material packs when available; earlier prop/structure installers preserve current building overrides. Generated media are excluded from the public code repository and require a separate local backup.

Validation: 323 backend tests, 103 frontend tests and frontend build pass. Behavioral checks cover inside-floor movement, crossing in both directions, corners and diagonal sight, attacks against occupants versus the wall itself, opening/closing/breaking gates, enemy door planning, T joins and rotations. Seeded map validation checks every spawn's exit route using actual movement rules with doors opened. Browser QA checks all authored layouts, asset loading, calibrated positions and a direct click on an edge-wall interior. Art coverage: 83 isolated encounters, 2,169 references, no missing files. Further material-specific buildings and player balance review remain future work. Production and live saves were not changed.
