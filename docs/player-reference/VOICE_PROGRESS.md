These are grunts, growls, pain sounds and dying breaths, with no written spoken
lines. Guardian is steadier, Strategist controlled, Opportunist scrappy, Survivor
wary. Female Goblins use a lighter feminine register with some rasp.

Applied to enemies in the audited road, Goblin, well, supply, worksite and prisoner
missions. Captures retain their voice key when recruited. Ordinary player avatars
and other races are not automatically given these voices. Weapon sounds stay;
unconsciousness uses hurt, not death. Rapid multi-hit vocals are kept compact.
