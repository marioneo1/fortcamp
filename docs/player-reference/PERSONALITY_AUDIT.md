# Personalities: what exists and what is missing

Audited October 9, 2026. **12 personalities are implemented; the 16 additions below are proposals, for 28 total.** Existing characters retain their personalities. This audit does not change saves, dialogue eligibility, or voices.

## Available today

Protector, Duty-bound, Steadfast companion, Self-preserver, Timid, Strategist,
Proud duelist, Opportunist, Berserker, Daredevil, Merciful, Curious explorer.

These mostly describe battlefield behavior. Only **Protector, Strategist,
Opportunist and Self-preserver** currently have the new Human/Goblin vocal packs.
The other personalities exist, but do not yet have those packs.

## Proposed additions

| Personality | What makes their voice and relationships distinct |
|---|---|
| Tsundere (`tsundere`) | Prickly about accepting help; expresses care through actions and reluctant admissions. |
| Kuudere (`kuudere`) | Cool, concise and emotionally understated; warmth appears in small gestures. |
| Dandere (`dandere`) | Quiet around strangers; becomes more expressive as trust develops. Shyness does not mean cowardice. |
| Deredere (`deredere`) | Openly warm, encouraging and appreciative; affection need not mean romance. |
| Genki (`genki`) | Energetic and enthusiastic; lifts spirits without treating danger as a joke. |

| Cynical (`cynical`) | Expects selfish motives and disappointment; respects evidence more than promises. |
| Idealistic (`idealistic`) | Believes people and institutions can improve; tested by compromises and setbacks. |

## How to make this work

- Keep **social personality separate from tactical behavior**. A shy character can be a brave protector; a Tsundere can be cautious or reckless. Reuse the seven existing combat policies where appropriate.
- Use one primary personality, with a small number of compatible history/relationship facets. Avoid assigning every contradictory archetype to one character.
- Anime influences should guide expression, not force romance, gender stereotypes, or a joke in every line. Devotion, rivalry and affection can be platonic.
- Preserve existing personality IDs and character history. New profiles should apply to newly generated characters or an explicitly authored development path, never an automatic reroll.
- Expand the authoring catalog and generated GPT brief **after the additions are approved**. Until then, imported content must use the 12 implemented IDs; the IDs in this table are reserved proposals.
- Create voice packs only for personalities we actually encounter, after auditions. No need to generate every race × gender × personality combination upfront.

The personal-story/radiant-conversation system remains a separate planned system;
these descriptions do not imply its quests or branching dialogue are already running.
