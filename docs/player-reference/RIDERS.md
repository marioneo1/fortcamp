# Riders

Boar-Rider Patrol enemies now have the **Rider** perk, retained when recruited.

- **Mount / Dismount:** Quick Action, once per activation; no equipped slot.
- **Mounted:** +1 movement; rider and boar both take 25% less damage.
- **Mount lost:** 25% hard fall (half mount max HP damage + Stun), 50% rough landing (quarter + Hobble), 25% unharmed. Control lasts one turn.
- **Boar Charge:** rider main action, three-turn cooldown; clear straight-line target at 1/2/3/4 cells takes 1.25/1.5/1.75/2x attack damage. Four-cell hits attempt one-turn Stun.
- **Targets:** click the portrait for the rider, exposed boar for its own HP.

Test a fresh **D-rank Boar-Rider Patrol**. The fourth layout includes unmounted
riders who can climb onto nearby boars. Other animal mounts and capturing animals
are future work.

Rider and boar animate together. Their effects share one map row, but each body
still has its own HP and receives its own statuses.
