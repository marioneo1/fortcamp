# Character stories at a glance

Updated October 8, 2026. **Two story designs drafted; none implemented yet.**
These are possible individual backgrounds, not traits every member of a
personality, race or Job receives.

## 1. The Watchful Caravan Guard

| Part | Design |
| --- | --- |
| Base personality | Strategist |
| Eligible Jobs | Fighter, Ranger |
| Eligible races | Human, Dwarf, Wood Elf; either gender |
| Individual traits | Reserved, aloof, protective, wants to improve |
| Hidden background | An Orc warband attacked a convoy they guarded. They now unfairly distrust unrelated Orcs too. |
| Introduction | Reveals distrust early. If the player is Orc, any planned loyalty ceiling of 80 is explained before recruitment. |
| Story | Conversations uncover the raid; a compatible mission may offer accountability. A separate trust path lets them learn to judge individuals fairly. |
| Possible outcomes | Combat specialization; reconciliation and changed future dialogue; or leaving the conflict unresolved. A close platonic bond is optional. |
| Reward direction | Specialization can offer an anti-Orc offensive perk or defensive technique. Reconciliation removes this distrust-based loyalty ceiling, without gifting loyalty points. Exact perks are not built. |

**Decision:** use a nonfinancial trust test rather than surrendering compensation.
Its concrete scene still needs writing. Reserve this specific story for one
character per player; other characters can share ordinary aloof/reserved traits
without inheriting its loyalty restriction.

## 2. The Workshop Ledger

| Part | Design |
| --- | --- |
| Base personality | Dutiful |
| Eligible Job | Engineer |
| Eligible races | Human, Dwarf, Gnome, Goblin; either gender |
| Individual traits | Sociable, proud of their craft, exacting, reluctant to admit mistakes |
| Hidden background | Concealed a counting mistake that wasted materials on an earlier job. |
| Story | Admit the mistake → agree to check work together → choose one preparation conversation → complete an ordinary mission → discuss the result. |
| Possible outcomes | Share credit openly, keep an honest private account, or stop the optional story. |
| Reward direction | Modest supplies or comparable gold, plus greater willingness to accept help. No custom combat skill or loyalty restriction. |

Failed missions preserve the preparation step; only the successful mission needs
retrying. Rewards happen once per player.

## How the larger system will work

- Characters receive stable individual history and traits. Conversations and
  real mission events reveal them; repeatedly talking does not reroll a person.
- Ordinary dialogue gives variety. Only selected conversations start personal
  stories, with limited active stories and remembered endings.
- Finishing a story changes relevant future dialogue without erasing history or
  turning every reserved character into a different personality.
- GPT supplies drafts and dialogue variations. Codex checks consistency, rules,
  rewards and references before integration.
