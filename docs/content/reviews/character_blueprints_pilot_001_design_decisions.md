# Pilot 001 direction decisions

October 8, 2026. User delegated the two pending choices to Codex. This records
chosen design direction, not runtime implementation or blanket blueprint approval.
Revision 3 remains preserved as received.

1. Replace the disputed-compensation waiver with a grounded **nonfinancial trust
   test**. Remove the proposed compensation dependency from the next working
   revision; retain retired IDs/history rather than repurposing them. Do not
   invent a payer or payment merely to make reconciliation costly. The specific
   scene, observable evidence, choices and deterministic recovery still require
   authoring. User need not choose the scene's routine details. No requirement
   for a capture-capable loadout or endless random mission farming.
2. **Reserve this specific cap-bearing story for one character per player.**
   Assignment must check and reserve availability before disclosing the
   cap-bearing background. Other characters may share reusable traits without
   receiving this story's grievance/cap package. Do not introduce a second
   reward-bearing arc for another recruit. Pre-recruitment rejection can release
   an uncommitted reservation; accepted recruitment binds this story instance
   to that individual, including deferral or dismissal. Completion or confirmed
   permanent closure retires the player key. Save/reload never creates another
   entitlement. These are planned rules, not changes to existing saves.

Before enabling the cap, settle how specialization or permanent refusal of
reconciliation leaves the relationship. Never advertise guaranteed future cap
removal while making its only resolving route unavailable. Prefer decoupling
ordinary nonreward relationship repair from the once-only specialization payout
if needed; that detail is not implemented or finalized here.

No new prompt round is needed simply to re-confirm these two decisions. Next
authoring should apply them and supply the missing concrete scene, without
commissioning bulk dialogue prematurely. The short user-facing reference is
[Character stories at a glance](../CHARACTER_LIFE_OVERVIEW.md).
