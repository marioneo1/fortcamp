# Fortcamp Race Ledger

Gender rule: Banshee and Dryad generate female only for new generic recruits and combat enemies. Other races retain their allowed profile genders. This does not rename or rewrite existing saved characters or named Champions/Celestials.

Rarity describes how often a race appears **when the party is already adventuring in an appropriate place**. It does not measure combat power. Minimum mission ranks and weighted regional pools make the rare groups substantially harder to encounter.

**Beastkin** is a broad rules category rather than a recruitable race. It currently groups Catfolk, Foxkin, Fauns, Harpies, Centaurs, Minotaurs, Lizardfolk, Kobolds, Merfolk, Dragonkin, and Werewolves. Characters always display their concrete race name.

## Regional logic

Ordinary missions use five recruit regions:

- **Frontier:** Humans, Halflings, Half-Orcs, and Catfolk.
- **Deepwood:** Humans, Wood Elves, Fauns, Dryads, and an exceptionally small Fairy chance at S-Rank.
- **Mountains:** Humans, Dwarves, Gnomes, Ogres, Trolls, and Dragonkin. Trolls and Dragonkin require S-Rank access.
- **Waters:** Humans, Lizardfolk, Slimefolk, and Merfolk.
- **Ruins:** Humans, Tieflings, Dark Elves, Slimefolk, and Automatons.

Regional events use their own populations. The Green Warhost favors goblinoids and large raiders; the Ashen Procession favors Undead, Vampires, Banshees, and Revenants; the Arcane Convergence favors magical and constructed peoples; the Great Beast Tide favors wild and bestial peoples; and the Starfall Omen favors Aliens, Astral Elves, Voidsent, Automatons, and Aasimar. Voidsent are outsiders sustained by aether leaking from ruined planes rather than ordinary undead.

Some critical successes can rescue or recruit an appropriate local character. A smaller set of missions contains hidden recruit encounters. Their required team compositions are intentionally undocumented. When a selected lineup qualifies, the mission analysis displays **“Something unusual resonates with this lineup”** without revealing the recipe or guaranteeing that the low-chance event will occur.

## Champion-only race labels

Champions can retain source-specific labels that are not part of procedural recruitment. Current examples include Elf, Half-Elf, Demon, Devil, Fiend, Hybrid, Soul, Arrancar, Witch, Goddess, Spirit, Dragon, Oni, Titan Shifter, Cyborg, Android, Immortal, Wolf Deity, Fallen Angel, Succubus, Esper, Lich, Dragonoid, Princess of Jahad, Awakened Human, Gifted Human, Red Witch, Mink, Skypiean, Visored, Humanoid Interface, Modified Human, Klaxosaur Hybrid, Raccoon Demi-Human, and Irregular.
