# Critical success and progression

Ordinary success pays the normal contract rewards and rolls normal loot. A critical is an occasional extra opportunity, not the expected completion grade. Mission-specific discoveries remain available on ordinary success; critical bonuses improve their drop chances without guaranteeing an item.

## Rolled missions and choices

Pass the existing d20 success check first. A separate seeded d100 confirmation determines critical success. A natural 20 does not bypass difficulty or automatically award a critical. A natural 1 retains the existing critical failure rule. Authored special criteria must still match before a critical can roll.

| Rank | Chance on a close success | Maximum chance on a successful check |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| E | 3% | 20% |
| D | 3% | 16% |
| C | 3% | 12% |
| B | 3% | 10% |
| A | 2% | 8% |
| S | 2% | 5% |

The chance grows gradually with the margin above difficulty and reaches its ceiling at 24 points. Early teams stay near the low end. Developed teams can return to easier missions with better success and critical odds, while each mission retains its exclusive loot. Even an overwhelmingly strong E-rank team has 76% ordinary success, 19% critical success and 5% critical failure under the existing natural-one rule. Percentages in the lineup preview include failed checks and exactly match resolution.

Dialogue uses the same bounded rule. A clean completed scene receives one finale confirmation, using the average successful-check margin. Adding more dialogue nodes does not multiply critical finish chances. Unchecked choices cannot create critical success on their own.

## Tactical missions

Authored bonus objectives still earn combat critical success deterministically. Killing the objective alone is normal success. Completing the extra rescue/capture/alarm or protection objectives earns the exceptional outcome; no random confirmation takes that accomplishment away. Combat loot remains separately rolled. Debug-forced outcomes remain available under their existing criterion gates.

## Scope and tuning

Existing accepted missions use the new resolution rule when they finish; completed results are unchanged. Ordinary success difficulty, normal loot pools, exclusive item drop rates and failure recovery are unchanged. Review real completion and reward frequencies after a trial before changing the caps further. Loyalty, economy and prison progression are separate passes.

Claim pacing: pools refresh every 30 minutes. E/D cost one point, C two, B three, A four and S five. Two opening waves provide five points apiece; free-for-all adds three plus upgrades (and ten extra while solo). The upper-rank ceilings account for their lower attempt volume. At difficulty 14 with total team bonus 10, overall critical odds are E 6.95%, D 5.85%, C 4.75%, B 4.15%, A 3.1%, S 2.2%. These are reference scenarios, not promises for every mission.
