# Martial Job art and audio

Dev assets, October 5, 2026. Created using the imagegen skill and built-in image
generation tool. Two consistent square 4×4 atlases supply the icons and effects.
Generated binaries stay in the ignored asset/staging directories, matching the
repository's existing media policy; release packaging copies the installed media.

## Assets and import

Sources: `staging-ui/martial-jobs-v1/icons-atlas.png` and `effects-atlas.png`.
Run `.venv\Scripts\python.exe tools/import_martial_art.py` to produce sixteen
128px square icons and sixteen 256px RGBA effect frames in
`frontend/public/assets/martial-jobs-v1/`. The staging manifest records every crop.
The effects require actual alpha. Tiny disconnected fragments are removed and
outer crop edges feathered; icons keep their square composition and aspect ratio.
The art was inspected as a contact sheet and in the actual battle renderer.

Icon order, left to right and top to bottom:

1. Brace, Second Wind, Victory Strike, Bloodfury.
2. Reckless Blow, Skullbreaker, Groundbreaker, Bloodied Strength.
3. Too Angry to Fall, Bloodthirst, Unstoppable, innate Fury.
4. Reckless exposure, Brace defense, full Fury, spent Fury.

Effect rows each contain four progressive phases: protection, restoration,
Groundbreaker and physical force. The final two Fury icons are reserved for
future resource UI; the current meter uses readable segments.

## Generation briefs

Icons: one square image divided into sixteen equal square cells, 4×4. Painted
fantasy tactical RPG ability icons matching Fortcamp's dark bronze, gold and
warm textured existing combat artwork. Each cell contains a full readable close
composition with no text or numbers. Protection gold, restoration muted green,
physical attack warm orange/red. Give every listed ability a distinct motif:
braced shield, catching breath, finishing slash, blood rage, reckless swing,
stunning hammer, ground-smashing fist, wounded strength, refusing defeat,
kill restoration, broken restraints, and clear resource/status motifs. No unused
space, labels, screenshots, UI borders from neighboring cells or explicit gore.

Effects: square 4×4 atlas on genuinely transparent background. Four equal frames
per effect row, with clean separation and no labels. Match the painted Fortcamp
style. Gold physical protection ring; restrained green recovery swirl; top-down
expanding earth/dust impact ring; warm red/orange physical force burst. Progress
from gathering through contact to dissipating fragments. Keep centers transparent
so portraits remain readable; no opaque ground panels, figures or UI borders.

These are successive painted phases, not skeletal animation or a physics effect.
Groundbreaker expands at resolved radial impact timing; other effects share melee
contact packets or self-cast markers. Short fades avoid holding opaque artwork
over the map. Reduced motion suppresses growth and aura breathing.

## Sound

Eight ElevenLabs effects are generated by the existing `martial-jobs-v1` pack in
`tools/generate_sfx_pack.py`; exact prompts live in SFX_GENERATION_GUIDE.md.
Raw MP3s and processing reports stay in `staging-sfx/martial-jobs-v1/`, processed
WAVs in `frontend/public/assets/sfx/`. The existing pack preview page includes them.
Reproduction uses the existing environment key; never commit or print it.

Brace, breath/recovery, victory contact, reckless exertion, skull impact,
Groundbreaker impact, defiance and restraint-breaking use warm, dry, short
physical sounds. Fighter care has no generic magic-cast overlay. Special impact
accents are quieter than the weapon hit, which retains armor/flesh handling.
Groundbreaker uses its own cue without Earthbreaker's leap/landing layers.

All eight final files were verified as nonempty 48kHz WAVs with no clipped samples.
This is technical audio validation; human listening approval remains pending.
No new effect library/runtime dependency was added.
